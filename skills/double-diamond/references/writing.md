# Writing and communication tasks

Load this for drafting or revising prose: announcements, emails, docs, proposals, posts, speeches.

## Discover before asking

Look for existing voice and constraints: prior documents by the same author, a brand or style guide, the thread being replied to, templates, and any facts supplied in the conversation. Match them rather than asking about tone.

## Decisions that usually matter

- **Audience and the action they should take.** If the intended action is unclear and the answer would change the whole piece, that is a genuine question. Otherwise infer it.
- **Channel and format** (email, post, doc), which sets length and structure.
- **Claims that need support.** Anything numeric, quoted, or attributed.
- **Sensitivity.** Names, personal data, legal or reputational exposure, and anything that will be sent or published, which is outward-facing and irreversible.

## Baseline additions for writing

- **Accuracy.** Do not invent facts, quotes, statistics, or sources. Mark anything unverified, or leave a clearly flagged placeholder.
- Voice matches the audience and the author; no generic filler or inflated claims.
- A clear single call to action where one is wanted.
- Privacy: do not include personal details that were not supplied for this use.
- Originality: do not reproduce copyrighted text beyond brief attribution.

## Phase 2 is rarely worth it

Use it only for a real positioning choice (for example which angle to lead with). Lenses are angles or structures, such as lead with the customer problem, lead with the result, or lead with the story. For routine drafts, skip Phase 2 and write the draft.

## Pre-mortem prompts

How could this be misread? What does the least charitable reader take from it? Is anything claimed that we cannot back up? What happens if it is forwarded without context?

## Plan shape

For writing, the deliverable is the draft itself. Present an outline in one or two lines, the key choices you defaulted (audience, tone, length, lead), then the draft. Offer to revise rather than asking in advance.
